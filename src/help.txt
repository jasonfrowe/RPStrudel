RPSTRUDEL
Strudel-style live coding for the Picocomputer's OPL2.

Ctrl+Enter     play / update (the change starts next cycle)
Ctrl+. or Esc  stop
Ctrl+S save      Ctrl+O open
Ctrl+K           delete the line
Shift+arrows     select (Ctrl+A all)
Ctrl+C, X, V     copy, cut, paste
Ctrl+Z           undo
Arrows, Home, End, PgUp, PgDn, Backspace, Delete, Tab: edit
